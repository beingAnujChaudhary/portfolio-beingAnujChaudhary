Executive Summary

The network is marginally above the 80% compliance target at 81.67%, but the outlet ranking shows a concentrated group of weak performers. Four outlets sit below 61% compliance, led by Nagpur – Civil Lines (47.92%) and Ahmedabad – SG Highway (48.37%). This points to a focused intervention queue rather than blanket network-wide action.

The current week contains 33 tickets. Of the 10 currently open, 7 are overdue; 5 are escalated and 2 are high-priority open. The key leadership issue is therefore not only ticket volume but the proportion of open work already past its expected resolution point.

3. Ticket Backlog & Issue Mix

The current queue reports 33 total tickets for the week, with 10 open, 7 overdue, 5 escalated and 2 high-priority open. The escalation table on the live dashboard is sorted oldest-first and surfaces seven-day-old overdue tickets at the top of the queue.

By category, Equipment represented 9 of 33 new tickets (27.3%), making it the largest category. However, category volume alone does not establish operational causality; ticket severity and resolution time should be monitored alongside volume.

4. Recommended Leadership Actions

Based on the data presented, the following actions are recommended for the upcoming operating week:

  • Prioritise high-severity tickets. Review the 2 high-priority open tickets first and confirm active, named ownership.
  • Reduce overdue backlog. Review the 7 overdue open tickets, assign a named owner and target resolution date, and escalate exceptions by Wednesday.
  • Investigate equipment. Use the 9 Equipment tickets as a starting point for root-cause analysis — without asserting causation from category volume alone.
  • Monitor execution. Follow up on the 59 missing scheduled submissions and watch whether completion returns above the 80% operating target next week.
  • Assign recovery plans. Direct Area Managers for the four outlets below 61% compliance to submit immediate corrective-action plans (CAP), with the fifth-lowest outlet monitored as a recovery case.
