Why some English translations need refinement

  • baden is a verb, so it means "to bathe" or "to take a bath," not the noun bath.
  • hören means both "to hear" and "to listen to."
  • machen can be "do" or "make," depending on the object.
  • fahren normally involves movement by vehicle, while gehen is generally to go on foot.
