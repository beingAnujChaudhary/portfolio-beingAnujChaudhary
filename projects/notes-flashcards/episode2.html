Tip: ei and ie reverse the sound many English speakers expect. Read the second letter as the clue: ei → “eye”; ie → long “ee.”

The two main ch sounds

  • The ach-Laut: /x/
    After the back vowels a, o, u, and au. (e.g., Bach, Buch, auch). Sounds like Scottish loch.
  • The ich-Laut: /ç/
    After front vowels and many consonants. (e.g., ich, nicht, Milch). Keep the middle of the tongue near the hard palate. Do not turn it into English sh or k.

Other high-value pronunciation rules

  • sch = /ʃ/, English sh (e.g., schlafen)
  • word-initial sp = /ʃp/ (e.g., sprechen)
  • word-initial st = /ʃt/ (e.g., stehen)
  • tsch = /tʃ/, English ch (e.g., Deutsch)
  • w = /v/ (e.g., wir)
  • z = /ts/ (e.g., zehn)
