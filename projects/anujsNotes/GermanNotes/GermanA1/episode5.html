Thirty Essential Verbs

These 30 verbs cover the most common actions you'll need for daily German conversation. Each verb is given with its du and er/sie/es forms (the forms most likely to vary), plus a natural usage example.

📖
Regular vs Irregular (Stem-Vowel Change) Verbs
Most German verbs follow the regular pattern (stem + ending). However, some strong/irregular verbs change their stem vowel in the du and er/sie/es forms only. These are marked with ★ in the table below.

Examples: sprechen → du sprichst (e→i), laufen → du läufst (a→ä), fahren → du fährst (a→ä)

💡
fahren vs gehen vs reisen
• fahren = travel by vehicle (car, bus, train) → Ich fahre mit dem Zug.
• gehen = go on foot → Ich gehe zu Fuß.
• reisen = travel (journeys, longer distances) → Ich reise nach Deutschland.
🧠
Quick Pattern Review for Irregular Verbs ★
Stem-vowel changes only happen in du and er/sie/es forms:
• e → i: sprechen, essen, treffen
• e → ie: sehen, lesen
• a → ä: fahren, schlafen, laufen

The ich, wir, ihr, and sie/Sie forms remain regular!
